---
name: openpond-training
description: Prepare or manage OpenPond experiments and model training, inspect retained results, compare compatible evaluations, or save trained model versions.
---

Read `connection_context` and the selected Model, Dataset releases and grader versions before preparing a change. Preserve user-selected resources. Discover actual readiness with `training_capabilities` or the selected method's capability tool. Source validation and a saved draft do not prove GPU availability or training quality.

Use experiment drafts for authoring and exact source/revision selection. Ordinary SFT/GRPO preparation uses `training_prepare_experiment`; Engram, Decision and selective-neuron work uses the corresponding typed `training_engram_*`, `training_decision_*` or `training_neuron_*` family. Follow each tool's schema and returned plan, source and qualification constraints; never substitute another adapter or backbone to bypass an unavailable method.

Preparing, funding preview, starting and saving are distinct operations. Inspect authorized credit/funding when relevant; a preview reserves nothing and a Billing handoff buys nothing. Launch only when the user has requested that workload and supplied the required scope and spending limits. Preserve the approved plan/price hash, operation identity and latest expected revision. A broad OAuth grant alone is not spending approval.

After uncertain delivery, read the original operation/job before retrying. Reuse its identity rather than creating a second paid launch. Use bounded status/events/log reads; disconnection does not cancel durable work. Cancellation needs retained lifecycle and cleanup read-back before reporting completion.

Read actual outputs and results before saving a Model version. Saving does not activate serving. Compare methods through compatible held-out evaluation manifests and exact scoring-pass results; do not compare unlike training losses. Saved Decision scoring requires existing authorized serving capacity and its own approved inference limits; MCP does not provision capacity. Link retained resources and distinguish measured results from unqualified methods.

For a bounded improvement study, establish observable acceptance criteria, permitted changes and total run/spending limits. Record each hypothesis, exact dataset/grader/model revisions, measured comparison and decision. Separate data/grader correctness from model quality; never weaken scoring or remove difficult tasks to hit a target. Keep training, development selection and untouched final evaluation distinct. Label grader changes as measurement changes and rerun comparable baselines. Stop on acceptance, exhausted limits, stalled progress or a required user decision. A failed launch without predictions is infrastructure evidence, not model accuracy. When continuing work from another chat, inspect the existing job/operation first and retain the remaining budget and stop condition; do not duplicate paid execution.
