# Riverside Community Pantry volunteer operations

## Arrival and parking

- Volunteer shifts begin at 8:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m. Volunteers should arrive 15 minutes early.
- The pantry is at 418 Willow Avenue. Enter through the blue volunteer door on the east side.
- Free volunteer parking is available in the church lot across Willow Avenue. Two accessible spaces are beside the east entrance.
- The nearest bus stop is Willow Avenue at Third Street, about 300 feet from the entrance.

## Check-in and clothing

- Sign in with the shift lead at the welcome desk and wear the provided name badge.
- Closed-toe, slip-resistant shoes are required. Wear comfortable clothes that can get dusty.
- Long hair must be tied back while handling food. Disposable gloves and aprons are available.
- Lockers are limited; leave valuables at home or keep them with you.

## Safety

- Do not lift more than 25 pounds alone. Ask for a cart or a second volunteer.
- Report spills, damaged packaging, pests, injuries, or unsafe behavior to the shift lead immediately.
- Wash hands before sorting food, after breaks, and after touching a phone or face.
- Never distribute dented cans with sharp creases, open packages, expired infant formula, or food with broken seals.
- In an evacuation, leave through the nearest marked exit and meet beside the community garden.

## Shift responsibilities

- Morning shift: receive deliveries, inspect and sort donations, stock tables, and assemble produce bags.
- Afternoon shift: greet households, restock tables, help carry groceries when requested, break down boxes, and clean work areas.
- Only trained staff may enter household information or answer eligibility questions.
- Volunteers must not photograph visitors or share identifying information.

## Breaks and accommodations

- A 15-minute break is available during each four-hour shift. Water and a seated break area are in the staff room.
- The east entrance, welcome desk, sorting room, pantry floor, and accessible restroom are step-free.
- Seated sorting, lower-lift roles, large-print instructions, and a quieter work area can be arranged.
- For an accommodation, contact Volunteer Coordinator Maya Chen at volunteers@riversidepantry.example or 555-014-8820 at least two business days before the shift when possible. Same-day requests will still be addressed when feasible.
